Who gives way? Cars and protocol, side by side

Illustrative animation: the car motion is scripted. The scene table, the plan (C backs off 7 m, B backs off 4.5 m along its own heading, A passes straight, B passes, C waits clear) and its deterministic hash 0x4F390A9A come from the planner in qnx_target/src/v2v_ttl_resmgr.c (host build and QNX 8.0.6 target verification). Every gap between two moving vehicles in the plan is at least 50 cm, the half metre that art. 100 (5) of the Taiwan road traffic safety rules asks of meeting vehicles (node visualizer/legal_gap_report.mjs). Without car A's frame, car C computes 0xE88DD551 and every car holds. Positions were measured on frames of the reference clip (evidence/scene_measurement/). Sensing is simulated; there is no radio link.
